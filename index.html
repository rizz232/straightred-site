What the app does

StraightRed is a desktop content workflow for football creators. It helps organize sourced story research, prepare original short-form football videos, review the exact finished video and caption, and send an approved video to the creator's authorized TikTok drafts.

How TikTok is used

The creator signs in with TikTok and grants only the displayed permissions. StraightRed uses basic account information to confirm the authorized destination and uses draft upload to transfer the exact reviewed video. The current version does not publish publicly or schedule a TikTok post.

After upload, the creator opens TikTok, reviews the draft, checks or adds the prepared caption and settings, and personally decides whether to post.

Who it is for

The staged desktop release is designed for football creators and small media teams who make original commentary, news and analysis videos and retain final control of publication. Creators interested in access can contact support.

Content and independence

Users must have the rights needed for every asset they provide or approve. StraightRed does not copy arbitrary third-party posts and does not sell TikTok account information. StraightRed is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok or any football club.
