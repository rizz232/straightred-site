StraightRed

A desktop workflow for original football videos. The creator reviews the finished video, then sends it to TikTok drafts.

What StraightRed is

StraightRed helps a football creator organize sourced story research, prepare an original short-form video, and review the exact finished video and caption on their own computer. It is a desktop app for people who make original commentary, news and analysis. It is not a public posting service.

StraightRed is independent. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok or any football club.

How the desktop workflow works

  1. The creator works from source material they are allowed to use and prepares an original football video on the Mac.
  2. StraightRed shows the exact video and the prepared caption for review before anything is sent.
  3. The creator signs in with TikTok and grants only the permissions shown on TikTok’s consent screen.
  4. StraightRed uses basic account information to confirm the authorized TikTok account.
  5. When the creator explicitly approves that video, StraightRed uploads it to that account’s TikTok drafts.
  6. The creator opens the draft in TikTok, checks the caption and settings, and decides whether to post. StraightRed does not publish the video and does not schedule a post.

How TikTok is used

Login is used to authorize the creator’s own TikTok account. Basic profile information identifies the destination account. Draft upload transfers the exact reviewed video into TikTok drafts. Connecting an account does not upload a video. Generating or preparing a video does not post it.

After the upload, the creator finishes the draft inside TikTok. Public posting remains the creator’s own action in TikTok.

Who it is for

StraightRed is for football creators and small media teams who want to keep research, review and the TikTok draft handoff on one desktop workflow. People who want access can write to support. The current release runs on the creator’s computer.

Content rules

The creator must have the rights needed for every asset they provide or approve. StraightRed does not copy arbitrary third-party posts. It does not sell TikTok account information. The creator remains responsible for the video, the caption, disclosures and the decision to post.

Policies and support

Read the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service. Questions and access requests go to redfoxgamesandmedia@gmail.com. Do not send passwords or TikTok tokens by email. Support details are on the support page.
