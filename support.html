Support

StraightRed is a desktop release for football creators and small media teams.

Email support
redfoxgamesandmedia@gmail.com

Before contacting support

  • Never email your TikTok password, access token, refresh token or client secret.
  • For a missing draft, check TikTok Inbox → System notifications.
  • Include the non-secret upload time and publish_id if available.
  • To revoke access, use TikTok's account authorization controls.

Data requests

Use the support email for privacy, access or deletion requests. Describe the request without including passwords or tokens.
