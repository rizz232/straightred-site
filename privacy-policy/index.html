Privacy Policy

StraightRed · Effective: September 30, 2026

StraightRed is a desktop workflow that helps authorized football creators prepare original videos and send reviewed videos to their TikTok drafts.

Information we process

When you authorize StraightRed through TikTok, we may receive the information allowed by the permissions you approve, including basic TikTok account information and authorization credentials. The app also processes the videos, captions, research notes and settings you choose to use.

How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate the authorized creator, identify the intended TikTok destination, prepare and review football content, perform a requested draft upload, maintain security and reliability, and comply with applicable obligations.

TikTok credentials

For the current desktop version, TikTok client values and access and refresh tokens are stored in the owner's operating-system credential store. Tokens are not displayed in the app, included in uploaded content, sold, or intentionally shared with unrelated third parties.

Sharing and sale

StraightRed does not sell personal information. Information is transmitted to TikTok only as needed to provide a function the authorized creator requests. Service providers may process limited information when necessary to operate the service, protect it from misuse, comply with law, or protect legal rights.

Content and retention

The current workflow keeps working files on the authorized creator's device. Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the requested workflow, security, legal obligations or the purposes described here.

Your choices

You may stop using StraightRed and revoke its TikTok access using TikTok's authorization controls. You may request access to or deletion of information associated with the service by contacting us.

Children

StraightRed is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect children's personal information in violation of applicable law.

Security

Reasonable safeguards are used to protect information, but no system can guarantee absolute security. Never send passwords, access tokens or refresh tokens by email.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the service develops. Material changes will appear here with a revised effective date.

Contact

Privacy and deletion requests: redfoxgamesandmedia@gmail.com.
