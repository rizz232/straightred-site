Privacy Policy

Effective: September 12, 2026

StraightRed is a football content management tool that may allow authorized users to connect their TikTok accounts and manage or upload football-related content.

Information we may process

When you authorize StraightRed through TikTok, we may receive information permitted by the scopes you approve, such as basic TikTok account information and authorization credentials needed to provide connected features. StraightRed may also process content and related information that you choose to manage or upload through the service.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate authorized users, provide requested account-connected functionality, prepare and manage football content, perform authorized uploads, maintain service reliability, and protect the service from misuse.

Authorization credentials

Access and refresh tokens, when provided, are treated as sensitive credentials and are used only to perform functionality authorized by the account holder. Users should never publish or share these credentials.

Sharing and sale of data

StraightRed does not sell personal information. Information may be transmitted to TikTok or other service providers only as necessary to provide functionality requested by the user, comply with law, or protect the security and integrity of the service.

Third-party services

TikTok features are provided through TikTok's services and are also subject to TikTok's own terms and privacy practices. StraightRed is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok.

Data retention and security

We seek to retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the service, security, legal obligations, or the purposes described in this policy. Reasonable safeguards are used to protect information, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may stop using StraightRed and may revoke StraightRed's access to your TikTok account through the authorization controls made available by TikTok. Where applicable, users may request deletion of data associated with their use of StraightRed through the developer contact method associated with the StraightRed application.

Children

StraightRed is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

Changes

This policy may be updated as StraightRed develops. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

Privacy or data-related requests may be submitted using the developer contact information associated with the StraightRed application.
